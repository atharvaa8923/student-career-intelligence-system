    How the score works

    Each metric is scaled from 0–100 across all imported candidates. Lower cost and time score higher; greater nonstop access and local share score higher. Your weights produce the final score.

    Capacity, availability, required activities and budget are feasibility checks. Unknown venue facts stay provisional. Equal metric values receive 50.

    Required columns & definitions
    city, attendees, local_employees,
airfare_total, other_cost_total,
travel_hours_total, nonstop_pct,
venue_capacity, venue_status,
activities_supported, evidence

    Use total round-trip airfare and person-hours. Nonstop % is weighted by traveling employees (0–100). Venue status: verified / unverified / unavailable. Activities: yes / no / unknown. A verified venue needs a source and relevant dates in evidence. Use the same attendee count for every city.
