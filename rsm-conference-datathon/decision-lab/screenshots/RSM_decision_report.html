Methodology

Alteryx supplies one aggregated row per candidate city. A LangGraph pipeline interprets the scenario, validates constraints, calculates deterministic scores, tests four priority settings, and explains the outcome. Cost and travel time are inversely min-max normalized; nonstop access and local employee share are directly normalized across all imported candidates. Constant metrics score 50. Weights sum to 100%. Ties are ordered alphabetically; near-ties should not be treated as decisive. Budget and venue requirements are eligibility gates. Unknown venues remain provisional.

Weights: cost: 15.0%, time: 60.0%, access: 15.0%, proximity: 10.0%. Airfare change: 0%. Cash budget: unlimited. Strict venue verification: no. Employee time valuation: $562/hour (reported separately, not included in the cost score).

Assumptions and limitations

  • Provisional shortlist: venue dates and required activities need verification.
  • Sensitivity uses four weight presets with current constraints. It is not a probability or exhaustive robustness analysis.
  • Airfare and travel hours must be round-trip totals weighted by employee counts. Other costs should include the chosen hotel, venue, transport, and catering scope. Missing costs must be documented.
  • Local share is a proxy for proximity. Nonstop coverage is not proof of seats or schedule availability. Candidate selection affects normalization.
